# duogt
DuoGT (Duo Growth Tracker) is a self improvement tracker for two people. You can compete, compare and work on projects together.

How does it work ?
This project was created with the intention of productive growth of you and your friend with collective efforts. You with your friend can record all your daily productive work (like studies, workout, reading books, learning new things, etc.) and compare it with each other. Points system is also introduced to maintain a healthy competition. You can track yourself along side your friend and keep yourself engaged in productive work. You can work on projects together. You can create projects goal, deadline and can log your work on daily basis. 
Track, work, compete and create !!! 

How to use: 
Visit: **Live link:** [https://veloxite.github.io/duogt/]

You or your friend creates a space. After creating, you will receive a code, share it to your friend. Once both you and your friend are connected, you can start tracking each other's work and can work together. The design is not highly impressive. You can always customize your own background for your tracker. Names and Logos are also customizable.
